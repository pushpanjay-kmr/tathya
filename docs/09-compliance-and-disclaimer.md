# 09. Compliance and disclaimer

This document is a project note, not legal advice.

## Current scope: personal use

Tathya is built and used by one person for private research and learning. It is not offered to the public, has no other users, charges nothing and is not deployed at a public address. On that basis, questions of research-analyst or investment-adviser regulation and of market-data redistribution are parked, not solved.

## What must happen before anyone else uses it

These are hard gates for any public, shared or paid version:

| Gate | Why |
|---|---|
| Regulatory review | In India, providing research, scores, ratings or recommendations on securities to the public can fall under SEBI's Research Analyst regulations, and personalised advice under the Investment Adviser regulations. A qualified professional should confirm whether Tathya's scores, verdicts, estimates and AI answers need registration. A disclaimer by itself does not settle this |
| Licensed market data | Exchange data generally needs a licence for display to others. See [Data sources](07-data-sources.md) |
| Source terms | Every data source's terms of use reviewed and recorded |
| Privacy | A privacy policy and data-handling review if accounts are added |
| Terms of use | Written terms for users |
| AI output review | A check that generated text cannot be read as a recommendation, plus a route to report errors |

## Product rules that apply even for personal use

These keep the product honest and make a later review easier:

1. No buy, sell, hold or target-price language anywhere, including AI output.
2. Rule outcomes are described as rule outcomes: "passed", "entered", "flagged".
3. Every derived value is marked as derived and links to how it was computed.
4. Every figure shows its source and "as of" date.
5. Estimates are labelled as estimates, with their assumptions visible.
6. The disclaimer is always visible.

## Disclaimer text

**Footer (every page):**

> Tathya is a personal research tool. Nothing here is investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell any security. Data is end of day and may be incomplete or wrong.

**Full page (`/disclaimer`):**

> Tathya is a personal project built for learning and private research. It is not registered with SEBI as a research analyst or investment adviser.
>
> Nothing on this site is investment, financial, legal or tax advice, or a recommendation to buy, sell or hold any security. Scans, scores, verdicts and estimates are the output of fixed rules and models applied to public data. They do not consider your circumstances.
>
> Prices are end of day, not live. Data comes from public sources, may contain errors or gaps, and is not guaranteed to be accurate, complete or current. Some content is generated by AI models and can be wrong.
>
> Investing in securities carries risk, including loss of capital. Do your own research and consult a registered adviser before making investment decisions.

**AI answers (appended to each):**

> Generated from company filings. May contain errors. Not investment advice.
